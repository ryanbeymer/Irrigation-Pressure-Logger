Irrigation Pressure Logger — Wiring Guide

Confirmed wiring for the working firmware: ESP32 DevKit V1 with an ADS1115 ADC, SSD1306 OLED, DS3231 RTC, HW-125 microSD, and a 0–80 PSI pressure sensor. For a color-coded visual, see wiring-diagram.html.

Voltage warning: the pressure sensor outputs up to ~4.5 V, but the ADS1115 is powered from 3.3 V and must not see more than ~3.3 V on A0. A 10k / 1k voltage divider (÷11) drops the signal into range. Never wire the sensor signal straight to A0 or to any ESP32 GPIO.

Pressure sensor (0–80 PSI) + divider

Wire colors: red = supply, black = ground, green = signal.

The 10k / 1k divider scales the signal by ÷11 (firmware DividerScale = 11.0). Calibration: the sensor idles ~0.437 V at 0 PSI, ~20 PSI/V slope. Constants are at the top of src/main.cpp.
