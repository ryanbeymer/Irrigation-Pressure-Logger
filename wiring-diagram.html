Before you power on — gotchas

  • !VIN, not VN. Power is the bottom-left corner pin. VN (3rd from top-left) is GPIO39 input — never power.
  • !MISO ↔ MOSI. D23→MOSI, D19→MISO. Crossing them is the #1 SD failure after power.
  • !Card must be FAT32, 32GB or smaller is safest.
  • !D5 is a boot-strap pin. Normally fine (SD holds CS high); if the board won't boot with SD attached, that's the suspect.
